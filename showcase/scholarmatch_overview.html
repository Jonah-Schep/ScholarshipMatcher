The problem
Thousands of scholarship listings, scattered everywhere
Spread across many websites
Some are scams
Many you don’t qualify for
ScholarMatch turns one student profile into a short list of scholarships they qualify for.
Meet the team
A manager program and six AI helpers
The Manager checks every answer
The Manager
Supervisor · ordinary code
Hands each scholarship to the helpers in turn, checks every answer, and sends it back with notes if something is wrong.
The Reader
Extractor
Reads one listing and writes down its rules, copying the exact words.
The Gatekeeper
Filter
Catches limits the rule book missed, and removes a scholarship only with quoted proof.
The Judge
Scorer
Rates how well you fit: strong, moderate, weak or none, and shows the evidence.
The Fact-checker
Verifier
Tries to prove the Judge wrong, without seeing how the Judge decided.
The Advisor
Chat agent
After the search, answers your questions about one scholarship.
The Essay reader
Essay summarizer
Turns your past essays into short themes, with your name and town removed.
What they work from
Four sources of knowledge
Scholarship library
Listings from Virginia Tech, USC, ECU, the SC Commission on Higher Education and counselor websites.
Rule book and calculator
Fixed eligibility rules, a scam screen and the scoring math, all written in code.
Memory and logbook
Past results, saved scholarships, chats, and a record of every decision the helpers make.
Answer memory
Ask the same question twice and the saved answer comes back, with no new AI call.
The AI helpers never see your name, contact details, town or school.
